the little fox lived at the edge of the wood, where the tall trees met the
open field. every morning the sun rose over the hills, and the fox woke to
the warm gold light. the birds sang in the branches, the bees hummed in the
clover, and the river ran softly over the smooth grey stones.

one bright day the fox met a crow with a shining silver feather. where did
you find such a wonderful thing, asked the fox. the crow only laughed and
flew to the top of the old oak tree. the fox waited and watched, and the sun
climbed higher and higher in the wide blue sky. by noon the wood was warm and
full of soft sound, and the shadows of the leaves danced on the ground.

in the afternoon the fox walked down to the river to drink. a small rabbit
sat by the water washing its long ears. the fox asked the rabbit if it had
seen the crow. the rabbit only smiled and pointed up at the oak tree. look,
said the rabbit, the crow is watching you. the fox looked up, and the crow
laughed again, and the silver feather fell slowly down through the bright
morning air.

when evening came, the moon rose pale and quiet over the hills. the fox
walked home along the river, and the stars came out one by one like small
cold lamps in the dark. the wood grew soft and gentle, and the fox slept
under a warm blanket of leaves. in the night the wind moved through the trees
like a slow quiet song.

in the cold months the snow came down and covered the open field with a
white and silent blanket. the river grew slow and still under the ice, and
the fox slept more and ran less. but spring always returned, and with it the
green leaves and the warm wind and the songs of the birds. the fox knew that
every cold night would end, and every bright morning would come again.

the next morning the sun rose again, and the fox woke to the birds and the
shining river. some days are for running, said the old fox, and some days are
for waiting. the young fox listened and nodded, and the two of them sat
together in the light. the wood was calm and kind, and the river ran on and
on toward the sea.

a wise fox knows when to run and when to rest. a wise fox knows the sun will
always return, and the stars will always come back in the evening. so the
days came and went in the little wood by the river. the fox was happy in the
warm light of the morning, and the wood was green, and the river ran on
toward the wide grey sea.
